# Aircraft visual references and implementation notes

Reviewed 25 September 2026. Models remain lightweight procedural approximations, with original Wing Lab liveries. These references inform silhouette and characteristic details; they are not claims of a dimensionally exact CAD reconstruction. Manufacturer/museum photographs are not copied onto aircraft textures.

|---|---|---|

Shared rendering changes: reflective tinted glazing, oval-window trim/highlights, differentiated roughness/clearcoat, subdued daytime navigation-light bloom, stable 16-tap PCF and blended overlapping terrain shadow cascades. These are real-time approximations rather than path-traced lighting or scanned aircraft assets.

## Animated controls

Control roles follow the FAA's [Pilot’s Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge, Chapter 6: Flight Controls](https://www.faa.gov/regulationspolicies/handbooksmanuals/aviation/phak/chapter-6-flight-controls): ailerons for roll, elevators for pitch and rudder for yaw, with combined elevons on the tailless Concorde. The meshes and hinge placements are illustrative approximations, not manufacturer CAD or a certified flight model. Stearman's upper wing stays fixed while its lower-wing ailerons move.
